Common questions

How do I delete my account or get a copy of my data? In the app, open You, then Your data. Export my verses gives you a file of everything you wrote. Delete my account removes your account and everything you wrote, immediately.

How do I report a post or block someone? Use the report option on a post to tell us about it. You can block a person in the app, and blocking takes effect immediately. We read reports and aim to review each one within 24 hours.

Is someone in danger? KaVerse is not an emergency service. In the Philippines you can call the National Center for Mental Health Crisis Hotline at 1553, or your local emergency number.
